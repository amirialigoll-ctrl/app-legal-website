ABOUT THE APP

Exam practice, on your terms.

The app supports question-bank imports, full mock exams, custom quizzes, exam-system presets, statistics, printable materials, mathematics rendering, and a study planner. Most study tools work on your device without an account or internet connection. Optional online features provide scheduled exams, results, question-bank sharing, announcements, and publishing tools. Google Sign-In is offered to authenticate users who choose account-based features.

The app supports 13 interface languages, including Dari/Persian and Pashto. The app is distributed through GitHub Releases and an in-app updater; the README states that it is not currently on Google Play.
